Privacy Policy — Funzo for Android

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Funzo ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App on your Android device, as distributed through Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

Funzo does not require account registration. We do not collect names, email addresses, or other personally identifying information that you actively provide. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

However, the App uses third-party services that automatically collect limited technical and behavioral data:

  • Device identifiers, including the Google Advertising ID, used for advertising and, where you have consented, ad personalization. You can reset or delete the Advertising ID at any time in your device settings.
  • Usage analytics (events such as screen views, in-app purchases, gameplay milestones) to understand how the App is used and improve it.
  • Device and app information (device model, Android version, app version, language) collected by the services below to deliver ads and analytics correctly.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address, used by ad networks for ad localization. We do not collect precise GPS location.

2. Third-Party Services

Funzo integrates the following third-party services that may collect data as described in their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising network) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) (consent management for ads) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics (usage analytics) — Firebase Privacy
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — managed entirely by Google; we do not receive or store payment information. See Google Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use the Information

The data collected is used to:

  • Display advertising banners and rewarded video ads inside the App;
  • Personalize the ads shown to you (only if you consented via UMP, where consent is required);
  • Analyze gameplay patterns to improve the App;
  • Process in-app purchases through Google Play's secure infrastructure.

We do not sell your personal data. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

4. Advertising and Consent

Funzo shows advertising provided by Google AdMob. At first launch, users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or other jurisdictions requiring explicit consent are shown a consent dialog managed by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can grant, deny, or customize your consent. If you deny, you will continue to see non-personalized ads.

You can review or change your consent choice at any time with the "Privacy options" button inside the App, when it is shown.

You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, at any time via Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

5. In-App Purchases

Funzo offers optional in-app purchases (cosmetic items, a scooter and a "Remove Ads" feature). Purchases are processed entirely by Google via Google Play Billing. We do not receive your name, email, payment method, or any other personally identifying information from the purchase transaction — only an anonymous indicator that an entitlement was granted. Refunds are handled by Google Play according to its policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Funzo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Analytics data is retained according to the standard retention periods of Firebase (typically 14 months). Game progress stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. You can limit the data used for advertising by deleting your Advertising ID or by denying consent in the "Privacy options" dialog.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or California, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction or deletion of such data;
  • Object to or restrict processing;
  • Data portability;
  • Withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect prior processing);
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address provided below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top will reflect the latest revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
