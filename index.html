Privacy Policy — Funzo

Effective date: June 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Funzo ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App on your iOS device.

1. Information We Collect

Funzo does not require account registration. We do not collect names, email addresses, or other personally identifying information that you actively provide.

However, the App uses third-party services that automatically collect limited technical and behavioral data:

  • Device identifiers (including IDFA — Identifier for Advertisers) for advertising personalization, only if you consent via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information (device model, iOS version, app version, stack traces) to help us fix bugs.
  • Usage analytics (events such as screen views, in-app purchases, gameplay milestones) to understand how the App is used and improve it.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address, used by ad networks for ad localization. We do not collect precise GPS location.

2. Third-Party Services

Funzo integrates the following third-party services that may collect data as described in their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising network) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) (consent management for ads) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics (usage analytics) — Firebase Privacy
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting) — Firebase Privacy
  • Apple StoreKit (in-app purchases) — managed entirely by Apple; we do not receive or store payment information. See Apple Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use the Information

The data collected is used to:

  • Display advertising banners and rewarded video ads inside the App;
  • Personalize the ads shown to you (only if you consented via ATT and/or UMP);
  • Analyze gameplay patterns to improve the App;
  • Identify and fix crashes and bugs;
  • Process in-app purchases through Apple's secure infrastructure.

4. Advertising and Consent

Funzo shows advertising provided by Google AdMob. At first launch, users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or other jurisdictions requiring explicit consent are shown a consent dialog managed by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can grant, deny, or customize your consent. If you deny, you will continue to see non-personalized ads.

Independently, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt asks for your permission to use the IDFA. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", advertisers will not receive your IDFA.

You can change your tracking choice at any time via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iOS device.

5. In-App Purchases

Funzo offers optional in-app purchases (cosmetic items and a "Remove Ads" feature). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive your name, email, payment method, or any other personally identifying information from the purchase transaction — only an anonymous indicator that an entitlement was granted.

6. Children's Privacy

Funzo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

7. Data Retention

Analytics and crash data are retained according to the standard retention periods of Firebase (typically 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crashes). You can opt out of analytics by denying the ATT prompt or by adjusting Firebase Analytics settings via the third-party tools they expose.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or California, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction or deletion of such data;
  • Object to or restrict processing;
  • Data portability;
  • Withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect prior processing);
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address provided below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top will reflect the latest revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
